Veterinary medicine × data science

Shared data to move veterinary AI forward

VETNET is building an ecosystem of live benchmarks for AI in veterinary medicine — any animal, any disease, any task. Launched at a 2026 Cornell Thought Summit, it unites universities, industry and regulators to create open, well-documented datasets, starting with CT and radiograph imaging.
