## What this draft aims for

- Text and controls meet WCAG AA contrast.
- Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link.
- Buttons and form controls are at least 44 pixels tall so they work on touchscreens.
- Pages use headings and landmarks so screen readers can navigate them.
- Content stays available when JavaScript is turned off.
