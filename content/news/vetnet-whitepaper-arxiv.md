“VETNET: Assembling Benchmark Datasets for AI-Driven Veterinary Innovation” is VETNET’s founding whitepaper. It was drafted at the June 2026 Cornell Thought Summit and is co-authored by all of VETNET’s founding members.

It lays out the case for an ecosystem of live benchmarks for AI in veterinary medicine, and how academia, industry and regulators can build standardized, high-quality veterinary datasets together.

The whitepaper is on arXiv as arXiv:2610.14149.
