Our research

Providing data for good research

Veterinary data spans many species, comes in many formats, and faces different legal and regulatory barriers than human health data. We work with partners to collect, de-identify and document it to one shared standard, so researchers can spend their time on science rather than data wrangling.

Benchmarks, competitions and papers

VETNET is designed as an ecosystem of live benchmarks: tasks that grow with new data, open competitions that compare methods fairly, and published results that make progress in veterinary AI visible to everyone.

Where we’re starting

Our first focus is medical imaging in DICOM format. We will extend to other data types as the initiative grows.

Now

  • CT scans (DICOM)
  • Radiographs (DICOM)

Coming soon

  • Free-text clinical notes
  • Tabular records
  • And more

Where it began

The Cornell Thought Summit, June 9–11, 2026, College of Veterinary Medicine, Ithaca, NY.

  • Day 1 · June 9

    What is, and what can become

    Keynotes from P-J Noble (Liverpool) on veterinary benchmarks today, Heather Whitney (Chicago) on lessons from human medical imaging, and Kilian Weinberger (Cornell) on datasets in the age of foundation models.

  • Day 2 · June 10

    Designing VETNET 1.0

    Group sessions on what VETNET should be, panels on ethical and legal compliance and on trustworthy benchmark repositories, and a first draft of the ecosystem framework.

  • Day 3 · June 11

    Whitepaper and public panel

    Synthesis and whitepaper drafting, then a public panel on the veterinary AI ecosystem.
