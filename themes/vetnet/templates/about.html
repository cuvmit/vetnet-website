Our mission

Why VETNET exists

VETNET builds an ecosystem of live benchmarks for AI in veterinary medicine — for any animal, any disease, any task.

Veterinary data covers companion animals and food-producing species, comes in many formats, and faces different legal and regulatory barriers than human health data, with no central body to standardize it. VETNET brings veterinary medicine, computer science, ethics and law together to create standardized, high-quality datasets and the benchmarks that let the field measure real progress — for animals, veterinary professionals, One Health and society.

  • Open by default

    Data and benchmarks shared as openly as privacy and ownership allow.

  • Rigorous

    Consistent standards, clear documentation and fair evaluation.

  • Collaborative

    Built jointly by academia, clinics, industry and regulators.
