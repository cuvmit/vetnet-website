Experts in veterinary medicine, computer science, ethics and law met at Cornell’s College of Veterinary Medicine in Ithaca, NY, from June 9 to 11, 2026, to define VETNET 1.0 and draft the whitepaper.

- **Day 1 (June 9):** keynotes on benchmarks in veterinary and human medicine.
- **Day 2 (June 10):** group sessions on what VETNET should be, panels on ethical and legal compliance and on trustworthy benchmark repositories, and a first draft of the ecosystem framework.
- **Day 3 (June 11):** synthesis and whitepaper drafting, then a public panel on the veterinary AI ecosystem.
